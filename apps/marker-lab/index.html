Marker & Fabric Utilization Lab

Arrange SVG shapes to explore possible fabric or material utilization. Uses the open-source SVGnest engine locally in your browser.

Prototype only. This is NOT an approved garment cutting marker: fabric grain, nap, checks, stripes, cut allowances, and CAD rules require separate verification.
