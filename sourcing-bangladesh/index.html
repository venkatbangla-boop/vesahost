Product categories for Bangladesh production

Bangladesh sourcing enquiries may cover cotton t-shirts, polo shirts, woven shirts, knit bottoms, joggers, shorts, dresses and denim basics. The right factory depends on fabric, construction, machinery, capacity and buyer audit expectations.

From enquiry to production

Share target FOB, quantities, size breakdown, garment construction, fabric and trim details, expected delivery, destination and packing requirements. VESA can then review sourcing feasibility, sampling expectations, time-and-action milestones and quality checkpoints.

Compliance, MOQ and shipping

Minimum order quantities, production lead times, certification evidence and prices must be confirmed for each supplier and style. Buyer-required audits and testing are factory- and program-specific. Shipment planning may include FOB Chittagong coordination.

Enquire about garment sourcing

Share a tech pack or reference photo, quantity, fabric and trims, target FOB, destination and required shipment month. VESA can then review the suitable sourcing route with you.
