About VESAApparel servicesBangladeshIndia

Knit and woven garment categories

Men's apparel includes basic and fashion t-shirts, polos, woven shirts, hoodies, joggers, shorts, casual pants and light activewear. Women's apparel includes tops, dresses, blouses, skirts, knit bottoms, woven pants and denim basics. Selected children's garments can also be reviewed.

Product development and sampling

The process starts with a clear product brief: technical pack, fabric composition and weight, specifications, fit, colorways, labels and packing needs. VESA can coordinate sampling rounds and buyer comments before production commitments.

Production and quality follow-up

Merchandising time-and-action milestones, trims and fabric updates, factory communication, quality checkpoints, carton packing and shipment readiness are reviewed against the actual order. Factories, quantities, costs and lead times must be verified for each enquiry.

Enquire about garment sourcing

Share a tech pack or reference photo, quantity, fabric and trims, target FOB, destination and required shipment month. VESA can then review the suitable sourcing route with you.
