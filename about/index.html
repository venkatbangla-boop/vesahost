Our garment sourcing approach

VESA works with buyer requirements, tech packs, fabric and trim specifications, production quantities, target FOB prices and shipping schedules. Potential suppliers are assessed against the actual category and compliance requirements.

Buyer-facing coordination

From the initial product discussion through development and production follow-up, VESA aims to keep sourcing steps clear, documented and practical. Commercial commitments remain dependent on individual supplier confirmation.

Leadership and sourcing focus

Founder Venkatesh Selvaraj brings extensive garment merchandising and sourcing experience. Bangladesh and India form VESA's two sourcing platforms. EGENVA-assisted tools support workflow visibility; final business decisions remain subject to human review.
