Apparel development in India

India sourcing can support selected fashion knit and woven programs, sampling, fabric direction, garment construction and value-added product requirements. The best route follows the actual technical and commercial brief.

Factory selection and costing

VESA reviews the required garment category, quality expectations, target FOB, shipment planning and supplier suitability before advancing any sourcing discussion. Factory capacity, compliance and price must be independently confirmed.

Two-country sourcing options

Buyers can review India and Bangladesh against different garment requirements. VESA supports discussions on sample development, manufacturing follow-up, quality expectations and buyer communication without promising automatic approvals or guaranteed production schedules.
